7.3 Educational Assistance Program

The Company encourages employees to further their education in disciplines related to their current roles or future career paths within the organization. To support this, we offer a comprehensive Educational Assistance Program.

Eligibility

All regular full-time employees who have completed at least six (6) months of continuous service are eligible to participate in the program. Courses must be taken at an accredited institution and must be pre-approved by the employee's direct supervisor and Human Resources.

Reimbursement Limits

Eligible employees may receive reimbursement for tuition, books, and required fees up to a maximum of $5,250 per calendar year. Reimbursement is contingent upon the successful completion of the course with a grade of "C" or better (or "Pass" in a pass/fail grading system).

Employees receiving educational assistance must remain employed with the Company for a minimum of twelve (12) months following the completion of the reimbursed course. Voluntary termination prior to this period may require repayment of the reimbursed funds.
